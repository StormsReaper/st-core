Request an official contract, complete it with the buyer beside the vehicle, collect both signatures, then submit the completed document to the DMV.

1Request contract
2Meet buyer at vehicle
3Seller signs & sets price
4Buyer signs
5Submit at DMV
Fraud protectionOwnership is transferred only after the named buyer provides a valid signature and submits the signed document at the DMV. The server re-checks ownership, vehicle and contract status before completing the transfer.
